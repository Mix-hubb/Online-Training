# Online Training

An online course website for Hidden Mines Co., Ltd. People who work in mining can buy short video courses, learn at their own pace, take a final test and get a certificate when they pass.

## What it does

**For learners**
- Browse courses by category, level and price (free or paid).
- Pay by card, PromptPay or bank transfer (with slip upload); prices in USD and Thai baht.
- Watch video lessons in order: the next lesson opens after 90% of the current one is watched.
- Take a timed final test with a limited number of attempts, then download a certificate that anyone can verify online.
- Ask the instructor private questions about a course and rate the course after finishing it.

**For instructors**
- See their assigned courses, grade written test answers, answer learner questions, manage the final test and follow their earnings.

**For admins**
- Create and publish courses, lessons and final tests.
- Review bank transfer slips, grade answers and reply in the support inbox.
- Manage the home page, categories and tags, users, partners and revenue share, the certificate template and learner reviews.
- Every admin change is recorded in an audit log.

**For partners**
- See their share of the revenue and the payouts made to them.

## Built with

Vue 3 + Vuetify on the front end, Node.js + Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL on the back end.
